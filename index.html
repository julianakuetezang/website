Note 03  The story

From the streets of Pretoria to the boardrooms of KPMG.

"My mother told me to work twice as hard to get half of what others have. She was right — as always."

Born in South Africa to a family who fled the Democratic Republic of Congo, Juliana was raised by a single mother and knew displacement, financial hardship, and a period of homelessness from an early age. She started working at thirteen to help her family survive.

In 2023, a Mastercard Foundation Scholarship at the University of Pretoria changed the trajectory. She earned a BCom in Accounting Sciences and a Postgraduate Diploma in Accounting Sciences — becoming the first graduate in her family — passed her first SAICA professional exam, and is now a Senior Trainee Accountant at KPMG on the path to becoming a Chartered Accountant (South Africa).

She's channelled every bit of that journey into opening doors for others: founding a foundation, building an edtech company, and speaking for the orphans, refugees and children of single mothers who are told their dreams are out of reach.

Note 04  The venture

Forgeforth Africa

Born out of the Anzisha FAST programme, an edtech platform connecting youth, organisations and opportunity.

EdtechAI-poweredFounder & Strategic Director

Skills in, opportunity out.

Forgeforth Africa helps upcoming founders and organisations manage their people — volunteers, interns and staff — while giving young people a place to track their skills, keep a living CV, and get AI feedback on what to improve next.

The goal is simple and stubborn: close the gap between education, technology and employment, so that talent from anywhere can be seen, hired, and grown.

Note 05  The mission

Life of Purpose Impact Foundation

Rebuild lives. Restore hope.

Co-founder & Strategic Director

A youth-led foundation serving the communities Juliana comes from — refugees, orphans, children in child-headed households, and single-mother families. Registered as a University of Pretoria society, it draws on a network of 100+ student volunteers and has impacted 300+ learners and 50+ families.

Its flagship project, "Equipping Africa's Future Through Education," earned recognition and grant funding from Global Changemakers — powering AI-literacy sessions, career-guidance workshops, scholarship-application support, and back-to-school and clothing drives.

AI literacy sessions Career guidance Scholarship support School-supply drives Clothing & blanket drives Lunch drive for the homeless

Spotlight project

Bridging the digital-literacy gap in Mamelodi

A university-led programme taking AI and digital-skills training into under-resourced high schools — training student-volunteer facilitators, then reaching Grade 8–12 learners before they hit the digital cliff at university. 30+ volunteers trained and 40+ learners reached so far, with a plan to scale to 1,000.
